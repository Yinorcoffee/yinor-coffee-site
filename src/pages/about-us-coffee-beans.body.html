About Yinor Coffee - Your One-Stop Coffee Supplier

From roasted specialty beans to coffee machines, capsules and drip bags - Yinor Coffee puts together complete coffee orders from Zhanjiang, China, working with vetted partner factories and handling the export side end to end.

Who We Are

Yinor Coffee | Zhanjiang Yinor International Trade Co., Ltd.
A one-stop wholesale coffee supplier based in Zhanjiang, China. We have four full product lines: specialty coffee beans, coffee capsules, drip bag coffee and coffee makers. With 10 years of experience in the coffee industry, we source from vetted partner factories and provide OEM customization with low MOQ. We verify import & export qualifications and cooperate with reliable freight forwarders to ensure secure shipments for our clients.

We are a trading company, not a factory - and we say so plainly. We do not own a roasting plant or a machine assembly line. What we own is the sourcing judgement, the quality checks and the export execution: we vet the factories, verify the food-safety paperwork and product certificates, cup samples and test machines before anything ships, and we tell you honestly what a factory can and cannot do. If a claim cannot be verified, we will not put it in a quotation.

Our four product lines work together as one complete coffee program:

  • Whole Bean Coffee - Regular espresso blends, premium blends and single origin (SOE) beans, roasted fresh in 454g wholesale packs.
  • Coffee Makers - Espresso machines, Turkish coffee pots and gooseneck kettles for home, office and hotel programs.
  • Coffee Capsules - Aluminum capsules compatible with Nespresso machines, nitrogen-sealed for long-lasting freshness.
  • Drip Bag Coffee - Single-serve pour-over drip bags in six flavors - no machine needed, ideal for hotels and retail.

We understand that our clients are not looking for "the cheapest ingredients." They want:

  • A flavor that customers remember and come back for
  • Products that add character to their menu and identity to their brand
  • Consistent high quality, batch after batch

That is why we commit to:

  • Strict sourcing - Specialty-grade beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, Brazil and Yunnan, matched to your spec and budget rather than to whatever one factory has in stock.
  • Roast development with our partner roasters - Each blend is refined through repeated trials before it enters your programme, balancing complexity and smoothness.
  • Transparent, competitive pricing - We quote from the factory that genuinely fits your specification and volume, and we tell you which factory that is. No invented factory background, no hidden layers of intermediaries.
  • Certification & QC - Food-safety documents and product certificates are checked per order; samples are cupped for flavour and machines tested for performance before shipment.
  • Stability & traceability - Traceability from green bean to finished product, with consistent quality across batches.
  • Customization - Custom roast profiles, private label and full OEM/ODM for beans, capsules, drip bags and machines.

Yinor - Great blending is not a compromise. It is an elevation. Whether you need beans, equipment or single-serve formats, we help you build a complete coffee offering that truly has a voice.
