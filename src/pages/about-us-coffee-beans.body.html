About Yinor Coffee - Your One-Stop Coffee Supplier

From freshly roasted specialty beans to coffee machines, capsules and drip bags - Yinor Coffee delivers complete coffee solutions from our facility in Zhanjiang, China, with factory-direct pricing and full OEM/ODM support.

Who We Are

Yinor Coffee | Zhanjiang Yinor International Trade Co., Ltd.
A one-stop wholesale coffee supplier from Zhanjiang, China, covering four complete product lines: specialty coffee beans, coffee makers, coffee capsules and drip bag coffee. We combine an in-house roasting factory with direct factory sourcing, offering custom roasting, OEM/ODM services and global export.

We started as a specialty coffee roasting business and grew into a full coffee solutions provider. Today our clients - cafes, hotels, roasters, retail brands and distributors - source everything from beans to brewing equipment from one trusted partner, simplifying procurement and keeping quality consistent.

Our four product lines work together as one complete coffee program:

  • Whole Bean Coffee - Regular espresso blends, premium blends and single origin (SOE) beans, roasted fresh in 454g wholesale packs.
  • Coffee Makers - Espresso machines, Turkish coffee pots and gooseneck kettles for home, office and hotel programs.
  • Coffee Capsules - Aluminum capsules compatible with Nespresso machines, nitrogen-sealed for long-lasting freshness.
  • Drip Bag Coffee - Single-serve pour-over drip bags in six flavors - no machine needed, ideal for hotels and retail.

We understand that our clients are not looking for "the cheapest ingredients." They want:

  • A flavor that customers remember and come back for
  • Products that add character to their menu and identity to their brand
  • Consistent high quality, batch after batch

That is why we commit to:

  • Strict sourcing - Only specialty-grade beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, Brazil, and Yunnan.
  • Professional roasting - Each blend is refined through many trials by our roasters, balancing complexity and smoothness.
  • Factory-direct pricing - Direct sourcing and batch production eliminate unnecessary intermediaries, giving you better margins.
  • Stability & traceability - Full traceability from green bean to finished product. Consistent quality across all batches.
  • Customization - Custom roast profiles, private label and full OEM/ODM for beans, capsules, drip bags and machines.

Yinor - Great blending is not a compromise. It is an elevation. Whether you need beans, equipment or single-serve formats, we help you build a complete coffee offering that truly has a voice.
