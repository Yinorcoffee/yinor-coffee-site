About Yinor Coffee

Yinor Coffee | Zhanjiang Yinor International Trade Co., Ltd.
Professional coffee bean roasting supplier from China, with an in-house roasting factory, custom roasting & OEM/ODM services, and global export.

Yinor is a brand specializing in mid-to-high-end coffee blending, roasting, and wholesale. We provide stable, traceable, and flavor-driven blended coffee solutions for cafes, hotels, and food & beverage brands.

We do not romanticize single origins. Instead, we believe that blending is an art in itself. Every Yinor blend brings together 3-5 specialty beans from selected origins - the floral notes of Ethiopia, the full body of Colombia, the natural sweetness of Brazil, the nuttiness of Yunnan. Under our roasting craft, these elements come together into a complete flavor story, making every cup memorable.

We understand that our clients are not looking for "the cheapest ingredients." They want:

  • A flavor that customers remember and come back for
  • A product that adds character to their menu and identity to their brand
  • Consistent high quality, batch after batch

That is why we commit to:

  • Strict sourcing - Only specialty-grade beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, Brazil, and Yunnan.
  • Professional roasting - Each blend is refined through many trials by our roasters, balancing complexity and smoothness. Every sip is full of flavor.
  • Stability & traceability - Full traceability from green bean to roasted coffee. Consistent quality across all batches.
  • Customization - Tailored blends and packaging to match your brand positioning and menu needs.

On the supply side, Yinor follows a direct-sourcing, batch-roasting, factory-direct model that eliminates unnecessary intermediaries. Though positioned at mid-to-high price points, we offer a specialty blending experience that exceeds expectations at a fair cost - making us a trusted core supply chain partner for premium coffee businesses.

Yinor - Great blending is not a compromise. It is an elevation. With our global blending philosophy, we help you create a cup of coffee that truly has a voice.

The Sourcing

We select beans from micro-climates where volcanic soil and perfect rainfall create unique profiles. Every lot is processed using advanced methods to preserve the cherry's character, ensuring our wholesale partners receive a product that stands out in the global specialty market.
