Who Are Single Origin (SOE) Coffees For?

Single origin espresso (SOE) highlights one farm or region - like Kenya Nyeri AA or Ethiopian Yirgacheffe - with a flavor profile that tells where it comes from. SOE fits:

  • Cafes that rotate seasonal singles to give regulars something new and worth tasting.
  • Menu storytelling - a named origin with traceable processing adds credibility to your brand.
  • Specialty roasters and importers adding single origin lots to their wholesale range.

Because single origins are more expressive, they pair well with lighter roasts and filter brewing, and they shine as straight espresso on a rotating menu.
