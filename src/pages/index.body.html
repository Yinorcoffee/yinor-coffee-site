FAQ

Wholesale Coffee Questions, Answered

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale products?

We offer flexible wholesale volumes for beans, capsules, drip bags and coffee machines. Contact our team for MOQ details, volume pricing and sample packs.

Can you put our brand on your products?

Yes. Private label and full OEM/ODM programs are available for coffee beans, capsules, drip bags and machines - custom formula, logo printing and package design included.

Which coffee origins do you source from?

We source specialty-grade lots from Ethiopia, Kenya, Colombia, Brazil, Guatemala and Yunnan, China.

Are your coffee capsules compatible with Nespresso machines?

Yes. Our aluminum capsules fit Nespresso Original-Line machines, with French Roast and Italian Espresso options available.

Do you offer samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available for cupping and product evaluation before you commit. Request samples through our consultation form.

Do you ship coffee machines worldwide?

Yes. We export coffee makers and kettles globally with full shipping and export documentation, including EU-standard plug options.
