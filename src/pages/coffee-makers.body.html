Wholesale Coffee Machine Buying Checklist

Five things to verify before you order coffee makers in volume:

  • Match the machine to the venue: hotels and offices do well with compact all-in-one machines with milk tanks; home-market retail favors simple one-button models; specialty cafes need durability for high daily output.
  • Milk frothing: lattes and cappuccinos dominate most markets - built-in frothers or removable milk tanks keep quality consistent without barista training.
  • Build and cleaning: food-grade stainless steel bodies and removable parts make daily cleaning fast and extend service life.
  • Voltage, plug and certification: confirm 110V or 220-240V, EU/UK/US plug type and CE or equivalent certification for your target market.
  • Branding: decide on OEM or private label early - logo on the machine, box and manual affects lead time and MOQ.
