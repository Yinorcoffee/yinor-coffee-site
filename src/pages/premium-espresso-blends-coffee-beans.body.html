How to Choose Wholesale Coffee Beans

Buying whole bean coffee wholesale is different from buying for your own counter. Three decisions shape most wholesale programs:

  • Blend or single origin? Blends deliver consistent, crowd-pleasing flavor cup after cup - the safe choice for cafes, hotels and offices that need reliability. Single origins showcase a specific terroir and work best when you want a point of difference on the menu.
  • Roast profile: Tell us how your coffee will be served - espresso, milk drinks or filter - and we will recommend a roast that performs in your setup. Custom roast profiles are available for private label brands.
  • Volume and packaging: Our standard wholesale format is 454g bags, roasted and packed to order. Ask about volume pricing, nitrogen-flushed bags and custom packaging for longer shelf life.

Every Yinor lot is cupped in our in-house lab with full traceability from green bean to roasted coffee, so your wholesale order arrives consistent, batch after batch.
