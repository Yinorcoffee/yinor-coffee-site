Capsules or Drip Bags: Which Fits Your Market?

Both formats serve single-serve coffee, but they fit different situations. Here is how to choose:

  • Coffee capsules are for customers who already own a capsule machine - especially Nespresso-compatible aluminum capsules, which brew rich espresso with crema at the touch of a button. Best for retail shelves, subscription programs and homes with a machine.
  • Drip bag coffee needs no machine at all - just a cup and hot water. Lightweight and individually sealed, it is the natural choice for hotel guest rooms, offices, travel, camping and gift sets.

Many buyers run both: capsules for the machine-owning customer, drip bags for the no-equipment occasion. Both are available with your brand - custom formula, bag printing and outer package design under our private label and OEM programs.
