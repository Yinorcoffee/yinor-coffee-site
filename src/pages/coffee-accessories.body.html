Choosing Drinkware for Your Coffee Business

Drinkware is one of the few parts of a coffee program that customers physically touch every day - and it is the easiest place to put your brand. Three things to decide before ordering:

  • Glass or ceramic? Double wall glass shows off layered latte and cappuccino and stays cool to the touch - a strong choice for specialty cafes and gift sets. Ceramic keeps drinks warm longer and carries color and texture beautifully, which suits espresso service and retail giftware.
  • Capacity by drink: espresso and concentrated coffee suit small cups around 80ml; latte, cappuccino and tea fit larger 300ml vessels. Matching cup size to your menu makes the drink look and feel intentional.
  • Branding and packaging: decide early whether you want custom logos, custom glaze colors or printed gift boxes. These affect both MOQ and lead time, so they are worth planning before production rather than after.

All our drinkware is available with OEM & ODM support - custom logo, custom glaze color and packaging - for retail brands, cafes, hotels and promotional programs in the European, Hong Kong and wider Asian markets.
