How to Buy Coffee Beans Wholesale from China

August 26, 2026 · Yinor Coffee

Buying coffee beans wholesale from China can cut your green or roasted bean costs significantly - if you work with the right partner. Here is what to check before you order.

Why buy from China

China's coffee industry has matured fast. Roasters like Yinor Coffee combine specialty-grade sourcing from Ethiopia, Kenya, Colombia, Brazil and Yunnan with modern roasting technology and competitive costs. The result: high-quality wholesale coffee beans at prices that help cafes and roasteries protect their margins.

Step 1: Define your needs first

Before contacting suppliers, decide on your roast profile, origin preferences, packaging size (454g bags are a common wholesale standard) and volume. Whether you need a house blend, a single origin espresso (SOE), or a private label program, being specific gets you better quotes faster.

Step 2: Ask for samples

Never commit to a bulk order without cupping samples first. A trustworthy supplier sends samples before you pay for volume. Compare them against your current beans - look for freshness, roast consistency and the flavor notes you actually want on your menu.

Step 3: Check quality control

Ask about their QC process. A serious roaster tests every batch, cupping each lot and keeping full traceability from green bean to roasted coffee.

Step 4: Confirm packaging and branding

For private label or OEM orders, confirm the supplier can handle your packaging design, printing and export documentation. Yinor Coffee offers custom roasting, private label and OEM programs with worldwide shipping.

Step 5: Test the response

Send a detailed inquiry and see how they respond. Speed, clarity and honesty about MOQs and lead times are good indicators of a reliable partner.
