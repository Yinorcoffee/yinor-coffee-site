4 Pour Over Mistakes That Ruin Your Coffee

August 26, 2026 · Yinor Coffee

Why does your specialty coffee taste bitter or watery? Even premium beans taste bad with the wrong brewing habits. Here are the 4 most common pour-over mistakes and easy fixes for home coffee lovers.

Mistake 1: Using pre-ground coffee

Coffee aroma fades quickly after grinding. Always grind your beans right before brewing for full flavor. If you buy wholesale, keep your beans whole and grind per batch.

Mistake 2: Wrong water temperature

Use 88-92°C for light roasts and 92-94°C for dark roasts. Boiling water causes burnt bitterness, while cool water leads to sour, weak flavor.

Mistake 3: Ignoring the coffee-to-water ratio

Follow the golden ratio: 1g of coffee to 15g of water for balanced strength. Too little coffee makes a watery cup; too much makes it overly strong and bitter.

Mistake 4: Skipping the bloom

Never skip the 30-second blooming step. It releases carbon dioxide and ensures even extraction - the difference between a flat cup and a vibrant one.
