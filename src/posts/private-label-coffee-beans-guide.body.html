Private Label Coffee Beans: A Complete Guide for Cafes

August 8, 2026 · Yinor Coffee

Private label coffee lets your cafe or brand sell roasted beans under your own name without building a roastery. Here is how it works and what to plan for.

What is private label coffee?

You choose the beans, the roast and the packaging design. A partner roaster produces, packs and ships them under your brand. You get your own product line - a strong revenue stream and brand builder - without the capital cost of roasting equipment.

Choosing your blend

Start with your menu. If you serve milk-based drinks, a balanced espresso blend with chocolate and nut notes works best. If you want a point of difference, a fruit-forward single origin espresso tells a story customers remember.

Roast and freshness

Ask for a roast profile that fits your bar flow and delivery distance. Freshly roasted beans with a roast date on the bag sell better - and justify a premium price. Look for packaging with one-way degassing valves to protect freshness.

MOQ and pricing

Private label MOQs are usually higher than buying branded beans. Be realistic about your monthly volume, and ask suppliers about tiered pricing - larger orders usually mean better per-kg rates.

What your supplier should provide

Packaging design support, export paperwork, consistent QC across batches, and samples before production. A full OEM/ODM partner handles all of it, from custom roast profiles to branded bags.
