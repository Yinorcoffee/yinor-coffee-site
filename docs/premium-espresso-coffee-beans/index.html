Who Are Premium Espresso Blends For?

Premium espresso blends are crafted from award-winning micro-lots for menus where the coffee itself is the experience. They suit:

  • Specialty cafes that want a complex, balanced espresso with a story behind every cup.
  • Roasteries and coffee brands building a premium house blend to differentiate from commodity coffee.
  • Hotels and restaurants positioning their coffee program as part of the guest experience.

Premium blends work beautifully as straight espresso and in milk drinks, and they are ideal candidates for private label programs where your brand name carries the quality promise.
