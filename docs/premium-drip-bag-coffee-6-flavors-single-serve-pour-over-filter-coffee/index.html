The 6 Flavors

Flavor Profiles

01. Black Knight

Profile: Nuts | Almond | Whole Wheat Bread | Dark Chocolate

Prominent dark chocolate and grain notes on the entry with heavy body. Finish carries dark-chocolate hints with subtle sweetness, zero acidity overall.

02. Encounter Latte

Profile: Vanilla | Roasted Almond | Peach | Maltose

Rich dry aroma with distinct herbal notes. Clean cup with low off-flavors. Roasted almond stands out, high fat content, zero-acid base.

03. Strawberry Monroe

Profile: Pomelo | Citrus | Cacao | Cheese

Great aroma with rich creamy hints. Smooth and clean-textured neutral cup. Ideal for drinkers who prefer low-bitterness & low-acidity coffee.

04. One American

Profile: Hazelnut | Cream | Chocolate | Toffee

Full-bodied with no acidity, well-rounded cup with lovely caramel character. Nut and dark-chocolate layers, finish yields cacao richness and silky cream texture.

05. Flower Queen

Profile: Jasmine | Lime | Brown Sugar | Milk

Intense floral aroma blended with bright fruity acidity. Every sip brings blooming floral sensation, delivering a clean, luminous taste.

06. Clouds and Rain

Profile: Dark Chocolate | Cane Sugar | Brown Sugar | Honey

Bright, soft acidity with well-balanced body and multi-layer complexity. Dark-chocolate, honey and cane-sugar linger on finish; brown-sugar notes emerge when fully cooled.

Drip Bag Coffee FAQ

How do you brew a drip coffee bag?

Hang the bag's ears on the rim of your cup, pour hot water (about 90-92°C) slowly over the grounds in stages, let it drip, then remove the bag. No machine needed.

How long do drip bags stay fresh?

Each bag is individually sealed, which protects freshness. Store them in a cool, dry place - sealed drip bags typically keep their flavor well for 12-18 months.

Can we choose our own flavors?

Yes. Custom roast profiles and custom flavor blends are available through our private label and OEM programs.
