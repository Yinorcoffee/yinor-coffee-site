Sunshine Orchard Espresso Blend (454g)

Wholesale Sunshine Orchard

Sunshine Orchard is a bright, multi-faceted espresso blend built for balance and complexity.

It combines the clean acidity of washed coffees with the fruity sweetness of natural-processed lots across Kenya, Brazil, Sidama and Colombia - expect berry, cream, toffee and chocolate notes with a full, syrupy body.

OriginKenya, Brazil, Sidama, Colombia
ProcessingWashed, Natural
Altitude1600m, 1600m, 2100m, 1800m
VarietiesSL28 & SL34, Yellow Catuaí, 74158, Cardua
Flavor NotesBerry, cream, toffee, chocolate
Pack Size454g whole bean bags

Available in 454g wholesale packs with custom roasting, private label and OEM options. Ideal for specialty cafes, roasteries, hotels and offices.
