Chocolate Nuts Espresso Blend - Dark Roast

Wholesale Chocolate Nuts

Chocolate Nuts is a bold, earthy espresso blend designed for richness and body.

Washed Colombians and Tanzanians bring dark chocolate intensity, wet-hulled beans add spicy, low-acid weight, and natural Brazilian coffee rounds it out with creamy sweetness - expect rich, smooth dark chocolate and nutty notes.

OriginColombia, Mantening, Tanzania, Brazil
ProcessingWashed, Wet Hulled, Washed, Natural
Altitude1800m, 1600m, 1800m, 1100m
VarietiesCardua, Typica, Bourbon, Cardua
Flavor NotesRich and smooth, dark chocolate, nuts
