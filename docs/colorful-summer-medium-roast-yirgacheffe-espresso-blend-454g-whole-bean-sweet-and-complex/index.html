Colorful Summer Espresso Blend (454g)

Wholesale Colorful Summer

Colorful Summer is a joyful, fruit-forward espresso blend for brightness and richness.

Washed Yirgacheffe delivers tea-like elegance, while Costa Rican honey-processed coffee from Las Lajas adds a syrupy, multi-layered sweetness - expect floral aromatics, mixed fruit and a rich, creamy mouthfeel.

OriginYirgacheffe, Costa Rica Las Lajas Estate
ProcessingWashed, Honey Process
Altitude2000m, 1600m
VarietiesHeirloom, Cardua
Flavor NotesFloral, mixed fruit, rich mouthfeel
Pack Size454g whole bean bags

Available in 454g wholesale packs with custom roasting, private label and OEM options. Ideal for specialty cafes, roasteries, hotels and offices.
