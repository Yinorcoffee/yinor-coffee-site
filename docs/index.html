FAQ

Wholesale Coffee Questions, Answered

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale coffee beans?

We offer flexible wholesale volumes for cafes, roasters and distributors. Contact our team for MOQ details, volume pricing and sample packs.

Can you roast coffee beans with our private label or custom branding?

Yes. We provide custom roast profiles, private label and full OEM/ODM programs - packaging design, branding and shipping included.

Which coffee origins do you source from?

We source specialty-grade lots from Ethiopia, Kenya, Colombia, Brazil, Guatemala and Yunnan, China.

Do you offer samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available for cupping evaluation before you commit. Request samples through our consultation form and we will arrange them.

How do you guarantee quality?

Every batch is cupped by our QC team and checked in our in-house lab, with full traceability from green bean to roasted coffee.
